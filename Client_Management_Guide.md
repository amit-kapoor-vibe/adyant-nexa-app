# Adyant Nexa: Client Management & Billing Blueprint 💼

To maintain your "Elite" status, you must be transparent but firm about costs. Here is the framework for handling billing, tools, and contracts.

## 1. Tool Costs: Who Pays?
The "Standard Industry Practice" for high-ticket agencies is that **Third-Party Costs are always borne by the Client.**

| Item | Who Pays? | Why? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ad Spend** | Client (Directly to Meta/Google) | Transparency. Client should own their billing relationship with platforms. |
| **Server Costs (Stape/GTM)** | Client (Directly to Stape.io) | It's their infrastructure. If they leave you, they keep their data. |
| **AI API Costs (Vapi/OpenAI)** | Client (Usage-based) | You don't want to get stuck with a $500 bill if the client's leads explode. |
| **Adyant Nexa Fee** | Client (To You) | For Strategy, Setup, Management, and Scaling. |

**The Script:** 
*"Mr. Client, our Investment Tier covers the high-level engineering and management. The infrastructure costs (like CAPI and AI usage) are billed directly to you at cost. This ensures you own 100% of your data and tech stack."*

## 2. Payment Lifecycle: The "Elite" Standard
Never work on a "Post-pay" basis for high-ticket services. It devalues your authority.

### Project Type 01: Systems Build (Website / One-time Setup)
- **Structure**: 50% Commitment Fee (Upfront) | 50% Milestone Completion (Before Handover).
- **Logic**: You don't start the engine until the first half is paid. You don't hand over the keys until the second half is paid.

### Project Type 02: Growth Retainer (Ads / Scaling / Marketing)
- **Structure**: 100% Upfront Monthly.
- **Logic**: Like a SaaS subscription. You pay on the 1st of every month for the work that happens that month.

---

## 3. Data Ownership & Security
This is where you win the client's trust.

- **The Policy**: "We build, you own."
- **Infrastructure**: All pixels, ad accounts, and CRM databases are set up under the **Client's Name**. 
- **The Database**: We recommend using a **Centralized Lead Database** (like GoHighLevel or HubSpot). We manage the automation (AI Agents), but the client can export their lead data at any time.

---

## 4. The "Elite" Contract Structure
Don't call it a "Contract." Call it a **"Strategic Growth Agreement."**

### Key Clauses to include:
1. **Scope of Dominance**: List exactly what you will build (CAPI, Funnels, AI Agents).
2. **Third-Party Expenses**: Explicitly state that tool subscriptions and ad spend are NOT included in your retainer.
3. **Termination (The 30-Day Rule)**: High-ticket agencies usually have a 3-month minimum commitment, followed by a 30-day notice period.
4. **Performance Bonus (Optional)**: For "Dominator" clients, you can add a clause for a % of revenue above a certain ROAS.

---

## 3. Communication Strategy (Keeping it Clear)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Use a Client Dashboard**: Instead of emails, use a Notion Dashboard. List every tool used, its monthly cost, and why it's needed. Transparency kills doubt.

### How to explain "API Cost is different"?
Tell the client: 
*"Think of us as the Architect and Contractor. You pay us to build the house. The electricity and water bills (API/Tools) are paid by the owner (you) based on how much you use the house."*

---

## 4. Onboarding Step-by-Step
1. **Invoice #1**: Retainer fee paid upfront.
2. **Setup Call**: Guide the client to add their credit card to Stape, Vapi, and Meta Ads.
3. **Access**: They grant you "Admin/Manager" access.
4. **Go-Live**: You start the Neural Scaling.

---

## 5. Agency Protection Policy (Anti-Scope Creep)
To maintain velocity, you must limit revisions. An elite partner doesn't "try" things; they "implement" what works.

### Revision Protocol:
- **Setup Phase**: Includes **2 Rounds** of Strategic Revisions. 
- **Post-Go-Live**: Any design/copy changes after approval are billed at a "Premium Hourly Rate" (e.g., $150/hr).
- **Logic**: This forces the client to take the initial review seriously and stops "Just one more thing" requests.

---

## 6. Market-Specific Friction Management

### The "India" Protocol (Boundary Setting)
- **Problem**: WhatsApp micromanagement at odd hours.
- **Fix**: Define "Communication Windows" (e.g., 10 AM - 7 PM). No business discussions on WhatsApp calls; use the official Slack/Dashboard for audit trails.

### The "US" Protocol (The Loom Strategy)
- **Problem**: Long back-and-forth emails and time-zone lag.
- **Fix**: Use **Loom** (Video recordings). Instead of a meeting, send a 3-minute video explaining the progress. US clients love efficiency and "Asynchronous" work.

---

## 7. Where to mention this?
1. **Pricing Section (Subtle)**: Under each tier, add "Includes 2 Strategic Review Rounds."
2. **Onboarding PDF**: Make it the first page of your Welcome Kit.
3. **Refund Policy**: Explicitly state that once the Setup Phase starts, the commitment fee is non-refundable.
