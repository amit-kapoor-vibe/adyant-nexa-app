# 📊 Pricing Strategy Audit: Adyant Nexa Marketing

**Objective:** Transform "Sticker Shock" into "No-Brainer Investment" using EMI psychology while maintaining Elite Authority.

---

## 🔍 Market Audit Findings
- **Mid-Tier Agencies:** Charge ₹50k - ₹75k flat. Zero tech integration. Focus on "spending" not "earning."
- **Elite Performance Agencies:** Charge ₹1L+ base or 10-15% of revenue. 
- **The Gap:** No one is combining **Custom AI Infrastructure** with performance fees. This is our "Moat."

---

## 🧠 The "EMI" Psychological Shift
Clients perceive a high monthly retainer as a "Cost." They perceive a high-value package paid via EMI as an "Asset."
