## 4. Logo Lockups & Usage Rules

1. **Primary Light (Alabaster / White Backgrounds)**:
   * Cobalt Monogram (`#0047FF`) + Onyx Wordmark (`#0A0A0C`) with Cobalt punctuation dot: `[AN] ADYANT NEXA.`
2. **Primary Dark (Onyx / Dark Mode Backgrounds)**:
   * Pure White Monogram (`#FFFFFF`) + Pure White Wordmark (`#FFFFFF`) with Cobalt punctuation dot (`#0047FF`).
3. **Standalone Monogram**:
   * Geometric forward-angled `AN` icon in Cobalt (`#0047FF`).
4. **App Icon / Favicon**:
   * Squircle (rounded corner container) with gradient fill from `#0047FF` to `#0038CC` with crisp white `AN` monogram centered.

## 5. Brand Voice, Tone & Messaging

* **Tagline**: *"Intelligent systems. Better business outcomes."*
* **Primary Headline**:  
  **Make business work smarter.**  
  *Make growth inevitable.*  
  *(Note: "Make growth inevitable." is always styled in `Instrument Serif Italic`)*
* **Sub-headline**:  
  *"Intelligent AI systems, automation, and intelligent workflows for ambitious businesses in the UAE and beyond."*
* **Tone**:
  - Institutional, sharp, analytical, high-status, consultative.
  - Razor-focused on ROI, speed-to-lead, revenue defense, and eliminating midnight dropoff.
  - Zero generic "AI buzzword fluff" — always backed by verified mathematics, operational metrics, and executive clarity.

## 6. Social Media & Creative Standards

* **Aspect Ratios**:
  - Instagram / LinkedIn Carousels: `1080 x 1350` (4:5 Portrait — max feed real estate).
  - Stories & Reels: `1080 x 1920` (9:16 Vertical).
* **Graphic Motifs**:
  - Flowing geometric wireframe wave contours.
  - Angled 3D architectural blue glass slabs.
  - Precision dot matrix background grids.
* **CTAs**:
  - Primary CTA: Black pill with white text + diagonal arrow (`Primary CTA ↗` / `Book a Demo ↗`).
  - Secondary CTA: Outline pill with subtle slate border.
