# Adyant Nexa Calculation Engine & Diagnostic Methodology (Validation-First V1)

**Version:** 1.0.0 (Validation Edition)  
**Primary Focus:** Indian Fashion & Apparel Retailers  
**Status:** Production Standard

---

## 1. Core Engineering Principles

1. **Deterministic & Explainable:** Every output metric is calculated using strict, reproducible formulas based on user inputs and validated Indian fashion retail benchmarks.
2. **Responsible Estimation:** Calculations represent *estimated indicative opportunities* and *potential profit leakages*. We never issue guaranteed financial outcomes.
3. **Graceful Handling of Partial Data:** If a user marks a field as "I don't know" or leaves essential inputs blank, the calculation engine handles it gracefully without breaking or inventing fake precision, returning `'Insufficient Data'` where required.
4. **No Fake Precision:** Calculated numbers are rounded responsibly (e.g. rounded to nearest ₹10,000) to reflect diagnostic estimation.

---

## 2. Fashion & Apparel Retail Benchmark System (India Context)

The engine applies standard Indian fashion retail benchmark distributions:

| Domain Metric | Fashion & Apparel Benchmark Standard |
|---|---|
| **Healthy Dead Stock %** | < 12% of total inventory value |
| **Healthy Gross Margin %** | 45% – 60% |
| **Healthy Discount Rate %** | < 15% average discount across season |
| **Target Repeat Customer Rate %** | 35% – 50% repeat purchases |
| **Healthy CAC / Revenue %** | 8% – 12% of total revenue |

## 3. Mathematical Formulas & Sub-Score Calculation

### 3.1 Composite Growth Score (0 – 100)

$$\text{Growth Score} = (\text{Inventory Score} \times 0.35) + (\text{Margin Score} \times 0.25) + (\text{Retention Score} \times 0.20) + (\text{Marketing Score} \times 0.20)$$

- Score Scale:
  - **80 – 100:** High Efficiency (Optimized Growth Foundation)
  - **60 – 79:** Moderate Efficiency (Identified Growth Opportunities)
  - **40 – 59:** Significant Leakage (High Capital & Profit Recovery Potential)
  - **< 40:** Critical Leakage (Immediate Cash Flow & Inventory Diagnostic Required)

---

### 3.2 Inventory Capital & Carrying Cost Opportunity

$$\text{Annual Revenue} = \text{Monthly Revenue} \times 12$$
$$\text{Dead Stock Capital Locked} = \text{Inventory Value} \times \left(\frac{\text{Dead Stock \%}}{100}\right)$$
$$\text{Excess Dead Stock Above Benchmark} = \text{Inventory Value} \times \max\left(0, \frac{\text{Dead Stock \%} - 12}{100}\right)$$
$$\text{Carrying Cost Leakage} = \text{Excess Dead Stock} \times 0.20$$
$$\text{Estimated Inventory Opportunity} = \text{Excess Dead Stock} + \text{Carrying Cost Leakage}$$

---

### 3.3 Discount & Margin Leakage Opportunity

$$\text{Excess Discount \%} = \max\left(0, \text{Current Discount \%} - 15\right)$$
$$\text{Annual Margin Opportunity} = \text{Annual Revenue} \times \left(\frac{\text{Excess Discount \%}}{100}\right) \times 0.70$$

---

### 3.4 Customer Retention & Repeat Purchase Opportunity

$$\text{Repeat Rate Gap} = \max\left(0, \frac{35 - \text{Current Repeat Rate \%}}{100}\right)$$
$$\text{Annual Retention Opportunity} = \text{Annual Revenue} \times \text{Repeat Rate Gap} \times \left(\frac{\text{Gross Margin \%}}{100}\right)$$

---

### 3.5 Marketing ROI & Acquisition Efficiency Opportunity

$$\text{Annual Marketing Spend} = \text{Monthly Spend} \times 12$$
$$\text{Inefficiency Factor} = \begin{cases} 0.35 & \text{if conversion rate < 1.5\% or untracked} \\ 0.20 & \text{if conversion rate 1.5\% - 3.0\%} \\ 0.10 & \text{if conversion rate > 3.0\%} \end{cases}$$
$$\text{Annual Marketing Opportunity} = \text{Annual Marketing Spend} \times \text{Inefficiency Factor}$$

---

## 4. Total Estimated Annual Opportunity (Combined)

$$\text{Total Annual Opportunity} = \sum (\text{Inventory Opp} + \text{Margin Opp} + \text{Retention Opp} + \text{Marketing Opp})$$
